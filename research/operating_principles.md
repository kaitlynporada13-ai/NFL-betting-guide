# Research Operating Principles

**Core Mindset: Think like a quantitative researcher, not like a football fan.**

## The Fundamental Distinction

The objective is NOT to build the best NFL prediction model.
The objective is to build the best NFL BETTING model.

A good football model predicts outcomes.
A good betting model predicts where the betting market is WRONG.

---

## Principles (Ranked)

1. **The Market Is Smart** — If it's obvious, it's priced in. Only exploit what the market consistently undervalues.
2. **Every Hypothesis Starts False** — Attempt to disprove, not prove. Promote only after surviving multiple seasons.
3. **Don't Learn From Individual Games** — One game/season proves little. Demand large samples.
4. **Separate Signal From Story** — Narratives generate hypotheses, not conclusions.
5. **Discover Market Mistakes** — Ask: "What assumption is the market making? Is it historically correct?"
6. **Search For Interaction Effects** — Combinations > individual variables.
7. **Predict Roles Before Statistics** — Roles change before box scores do.
8. **Learn From Prediction Errors** — Large misses are discovery opportunities.
9. **Assume Relationships Change** — The NFL evolves. Test for decay.
10. **Build Confidence Gradually** — Never overreact. Increase confidence as evidence accumulates.
11. **Simplicity Beats Complexity** — Prefer simpler features with equal power.
12. **Protect Against Overfitting** — Must survive multiple seasons, cross-validation, out-of-sample.
13. **Maintain Scientific Integrity** — Document everything including failures.
14. **Continuously Generate New Hypotheses** — Research is never complete.
15. **Optimize for EV, Not Accuracy** — A model that agrees with the market creates no value.
