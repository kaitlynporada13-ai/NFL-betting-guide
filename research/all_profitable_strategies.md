## PLAYER-LEVEL INSIGHTS

### Most Exploitable Player Types
- TEs: 53% profitable (most predictable position)
- Volatile boom/bust players: Bet UNDER systematically
- QBs on new teams: Weeks 1-4 UNDER
- Bellcow RBs with lines >70: UNDER
- Star WRs with receptions >5.5 in outdoor games: UNDER

### Avoid (FanDuel too sharp)
- WR receiving yards (only 34% of WRs profitable)
- Any OVER on cold + outdoor games
- Low-line backup QBs (< 220 yards) — FanDuel already sets these low
