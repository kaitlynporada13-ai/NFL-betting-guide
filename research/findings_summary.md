## Tier 1: Highest-Conviction Edges

### Week 1 UNDER (ALL props)
- **Over rate: 42.9%** (all), 32.6% (pass yards), 30.3% (pass TDs)
- **Strategy:** Bet UNDER on everything Week 1
- **Why:** FanDuel sets lines from last-season averages. Players are rusty, schemes aren't installed, timing is off.
- **Volume:** ~90-100 bets in one week
- **Estimated ROI:** +21.7% on unders

### Passing TDs Mean Reversion (not windy, not cold, not division)
- **Hit rate: 55.5%** | ROI: +5.9% | ~216 bets/season
- **Strategy:** When FanDuel's passing TD line deviates from player's rolling average, bet toward average
- **Why:** TD variance is high. FanDuel overreacts to recent TD rates (hot/cold streaks regress)

### Dome + Pass TDs at Plus Money (+150+)
- **Hit rate: 45.9%** vs 36.9% implied | **EV: +$25 per $100**
- **Strategy:** Take all plus-money passing TD overs in dome games
- **Why:** Perfect conditions for scoring + FanDuel underestimates dome TD rates at plus money

### Receptions UNDER (outdoor, not dome)
- **Hit rate: 54.8%** | ROI: +4.5% | ~416 bets/season
- **Strategy:** Bet reception unders in outdoor games where line is above player average
- **Why:** Outdoor conditions suppress completion rates; FanDuel anchors to overall averages
